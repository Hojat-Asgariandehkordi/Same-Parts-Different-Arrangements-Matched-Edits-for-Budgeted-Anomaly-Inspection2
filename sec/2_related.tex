\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
MVTec LOCO introduced anomalies that violate constraints on the count, position or combination of otherwise normal parts \cite{bergmann2022}. Appearance-based detectors such as its GCAD baseline, EfficientAD, PatchCore and Gaussian feature models detect many of them \cite{bergmann2022,batzner2024,roth2022,rippel2021}. Component-based methods segment the scene into parts and compare part attributes, histograms or correspondences \cite{liu2023,kim2024psad,hsieh2024csad,peng2025}, and vision--language and rule-based systems reason about explicit constraints \cite{jeong2023winclip,zhou2024anomalyclip,zhang2025logsad,ji2026,zhang2026logico}. SALAD adds a composition branch that reads a part-segmentation map and predicts an anomaly map trained on synthetic edits of that map \cite{fucka2025}. Synthetic defects are the common source of localization supervision across these families: cut-and-paste patches, Perlin-masked textures, blended natural patches, generated defects and synthesized logical defects \cite{li2021cutpaste,zavrtanik2021draem,schluter2022nsa,hu2024anomalydiffusion,zhao2024}. This line of work varies how a synthetic defect looks. We study the spatial extent of its label, which SALAD deliberately sets to a whole class for insertions \cite{fucka2025}, and show that it determines where the map points.

Pixel AUROC and per-region overlap summarize a map over all thresholds \cite{bergmann2021ijcv}. MVTec LOCO's sPRO saturates each defect region at a defect-specific area to accommodate ambiguous logical defects and integrates only up to a low false-positive rate \cite{bergmann2022}. These curves measure what a map can rank; deployed systems instead fix their cutoffs in advance, often with conformal guarantees on normal data \cite{bates2023,angelopoulos2023}. We complement sPRO with costs at fixed operating points in the unit an inspector opens. Our edits follow the logic of minimal pairs, which isolate one factor while holding others fixed in contrast sets, counterfactually augmented data and Winoground \cite{gardner2020contrast,kaushik2020counterfactual,thrush2022}, and of sanity checks that ask whether a map changes when, and only when, its cause changes \cite{adebayo2018sanity}.
